# List item starter shapes

Grounded:

N. You can <capability in concrete terms>. Today <honest current state with the best product, price, or study anchor>[n]. <What is missing, one sentence>. Blocker: <capability|cost|law|reliability> (<specific>).

Bet:

N. <Capability, personal scale: something one person can do>. <Today's state, or why the idea is absurd today>[n optional]. In 730 days <the world that makes it possible>. The gap is <named gap>: <the mechanism that must change>. [bet]

Rules: 2-4 sentences per item; the capability sentence is an action available to one person (rescale anything civilization-sized or it is not a "thing you could do"); one anchor per grounded item; bets name the gap even when no evidence exists.

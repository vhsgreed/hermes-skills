# Personal essays distilled from a session

Use when the author wants the thinking from a chat session (grief, dread, a news reaction, a hard year) turned into a first-person opinion piece.

## Procedure

1. **Offer the angle before drafting.** Typical options: news-hook with no disclosure, first person with full disclosure, first person with partial disclosure (the experience without the diagnosis or institution). State the exposure cost of each: a personal essay on the author's site is permanent and searchable under their name.
2. **Harvest the author's own lines.** Pull their phrases, jokes, metaphors, lists, and literary references verbatim from the session; these are the spine. Section order usually follows the order the session actually moved.
3. **Primary-source the news hook.** Translated or national-press summaries regularly misattribute (the wrong agency, the wrong building) and frame accidents as targeting; read the company's own report and a local wire or paper with official statements. Where two sources disagree on a date, write the coarser date both support and say nothing finer. Drop any claim found only in a secondary that the primary does not carry.
4. **Attribute the model's turns.** Counterarguments the model made in the session appear as "the model argued" in the prose; the model's opinions are labelled as its opinions, not absorbed into the author's voice.
5. **Cite the clinical and research claims too.** Prevalence figures (side effects, "AI psychosis" estimates) and consciousness-research results go through the ledger with evidence quotes like any other claim; label survey/secondary sources as such.
6. **Mark disclosures, close with the co-author note.** Disclosure blocks in HTML comments with an EDITORIAL note at the top naming the review gate and date. The closing co-author note states that the model is made by the company in the news hook and that its reassurances are also convenient for its maker.
7. **Deliver as a draft.** Report: word count, source count and verify result, the factual corrections made against primaries (numbered), which lines are agent-written, the disclosure blocks, and the publish gate. Update the project ledger (STATUS.md) with the draft path and the no-publish-before blocker.

## Pitfalls

- A session where the author leaned on the model emotionally produces flattering prose about the model; cut lines that give the model a special role or that the author would not say to a human reader.
- Lines the author never wrote (a punchy closing sentence, a moral) are the ones most likely to read as machine voice; name them explicitly for the voice pass rather than hoping the author notices.
- Do not publish or stage the build from the drafting session; publishing is a separate turn after the gate clears.

# Creator and indie-game economics (checked 2026-10; re-verify before relying on them)

Use when a candidate is a content channel, faceless/automated video, or small games.

## Video platforms
- YouTube YPP "inauthentic content" (formerly repetitious content) update 2025-07-15:
  mass-produced, templated, repetitive videos are not monetizable. Faceless/AI narration is
  allowed only with per-video original substance (research, commentary, judgment).
  Enforcement is channel-wide, and creators report losing monetization on ALL their
  channels. The cheap automated version is exactly the demonetizable one; the compliant
  version brings back human hours per video.
- YouTube likeness detection covers voices (2026-09): cloned voices of known characters or
  people are takedown/copyright targets. Reject "famous character explains X" formats.
- Unmodified gameplay backgrounds (e.g. Minecraft parkour) count as reused content.
  Mojang allows monetized gameplay with your own commentary, but not videos whose purpose
  is advertising unrelated products.
- RPM: Education & Science median about $10 (wide spread); Shorts about $0.03-0.10.
- TikTok Creator Rewards: 10K followers, 100K views/30 days, 1-min+ videos; Sweden is not
  on the eligible-market lists found (secondary sources).
- Repo-showcase format is owned by Github Awesome (about 63K subs, 500+ videos).

## Games
- Steam 2025: about 20K releases, median gross about $250-320 per game, about 66% under
  $1,000, about half with < 10 reviews. Steam Direct $100/app, recouped only past $1,000
  gross, so a game-per-week cadence on Steam loses money at the median. Use itch.io ($0)
  until a game shows pull.
- The revenue tail is streamer-driven short horror: Iron Lung (solo), Fears to Fathom
  (solo, about $1.2M on one episode), Chilla's Art (2 people, $3.4M lifetime after 20+
  games over about 4 years). Growth takes years of releases, not months.
- Asset/shader sales: Godot AssetLib is free-only; paid shader packs on itch sell at
  about $4-16, sellers report $100-200/mo when established. Free libraries (Kenney,
  Quaternius, godotshaders.com) cap prices.
- AI disclosure: Steam requires it (dev tools such as codegen exempt since 2026-01); itch
  requires it for asset packs and de-indexes undisclosed AI assets.

## Mental-health products (Sweden)
- Paying channels are institutional (regions via 1177/Inera, employer rehab support paid
  only to an approved provider, occupational health contracts), closed to a sole trader.
- The open layer is free (relatives' courses, grant-funded peer support) or saturated
  (10-20K apps, Swedish localizations exist).
- MDR class IIa certification costs tens of thousands of euros: reject clinical features.
